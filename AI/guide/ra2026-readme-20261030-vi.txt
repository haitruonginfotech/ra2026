RA2026 - BAN PORTABLE (KHONG GHI RA NGOAI THU MUC)
==================================================

CACH CHOI
  - Red Alert 2      : chay  Play_RA2.exe
  - Yuri's Revenge   : chay  Play_YR.exe
  (Co the tao shortcut ra Desktop. KHONG chay truc tiep game.exe/gamemd.exe:
   game se tu thoat vi thieu "mutex" cua trinh mo.)

CO CHE BAO VE
  BINKW32.DLL (thu vien video, ban goc doi ten thanh binkw32_o.dll) duoc thay
  bang mot DLL nho, nap vao game moi lan chay. No:
   - Cho game dung REGISTRY AO: moi thu game "ghi" vao registry nam trong
     portable\registry.txt. Doc thi xem registry ao truoc, roi doc registry
     that o che do CHI DOC. Registry Windows khong bi ghi gi.
   - Chan moi lenh ghi/tao/xoa/doi ten file BEN NGOAI thu muc Ra2026
     (ke ca ghi vao C:\Windows, win.ini). Moi lan chan duoc ghi vao
     portable\blocked.log.
  Play_RA2.exe / Play_YR.exe chi tao mutex va mo game, khong ghi gi.
  Ma nguon: thu muc _portable_src.

DA LOAI BO SO VOI BAN GOC
  keygen\, LAN-play-serials\ (*.reg), RegSetup.exe, SetupReg.exe,
  uninstal.exe/.ini, DRVMGT.DLL (driver SafeDisc), PATCHGET.DAT,
  patchgetmd.dat, PATCHW32.DLL, src.7z, aqrit.cfg,
  Ra2.exe / RA2MD.exe / YURI.exe (trinh mo goc - co ghi registry).
  "cnc-ddraw config.exe" doi ten thanh .disabled (cong cu nay ghi registry);
  chinh do hoa bang cach sua ddraw.ini.

KHONG CHAN DUOC (do Windows tu ghi, khong phai game)
  Prefetch, lich su mo chuong trinh (UserAssist/MUICache), quy tac tuong
  lua neu ban bam "Allow" khi choi LAN, cai dat tuong thich neu ban tu
  dat Compatibility mode.
